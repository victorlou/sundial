# Sundial

A mobile app for one household's routines and chores. Tasks recur on flexible schedules, sit in the parts of the day you choose, and work fully offline. A household can share some of them, and kids get a board of their own.

It's built in SwiftUI, with a Supabase + PowerSync backend for sync.

## Status

P0 (foundations) is done. P1, a solo app with no account or sync, is next. The phases and what each includes are in the [roadmap](docs/05-roadmap.md).
